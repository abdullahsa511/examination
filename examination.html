General Instructions

  • The clock at the top right will display the remaining time. The exam will auto-submit when the timer reaches zero.
  • The Question Palette on the right shows the status of each question using symbols and colors.
  • Click on a question number to go to that question directly.
  • Select one answer by clicking on the option. To deselect, click Clear Response.
  • To mark a question for review, click Mark for Review. Answers marked for review will be evaluated.
  • Negative marking of −0.25 applies for every incorrect answer.
  • You can navigate between questions freely using the Previous / Next buttons or the palette.
  • To submit the exam, click Submit Examination. A confirmation will appear.
  • Once submitted, you cannot change any answer.
  • Do not refresh or close the browser during the exam. Your progress is auto-saved.

⚠️ Important Rules

  • Any form of unfair means will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Use of mobile phones, calculators, or external help is strictly prohibited.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection throughout the exam.
